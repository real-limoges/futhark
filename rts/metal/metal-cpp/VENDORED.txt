Vendored copy of metal-cpp (Apple's C++ bindings for Metal).

Source:  https://github.com/bkaradzic/metal-cpp
         (mirror of Apple's official metal-cpp releases)
Commit:  c9727bc9468a90d7ea8fc89d5ee03b8d8992a570
Release: "metal-cpp for macOS27, iOS27"
Date vendored: 2026-07-11

Only the header trees Foundation/, Metal/ and QuartzCore/ (plus
LICENSE.txt and README.md) are vendored; samples, SingleHeader/ and
MetalFX/ are omitted.  See rts/metal/DESIGN.md for how these headers
are located at build time (-I, overridable via FUTHARK_METAL_CPP).
